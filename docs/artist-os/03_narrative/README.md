# 03 Narrative — Detailed Product Specification

**Status:** REVIEW complete — first detailed product pass.

Narrative is the audience-facing storytelling layer between the artist's internal Identity Narrative and concrete Content execution.

## Core conceptual boundary

```text
Identity Narrative
= What is the larger internal artistic world/story?

Narrative Track
= Which part of that larger story are we telling over time?

Narrative Beat
= What concrete story development/chapter step comes next?

Content Pillar
= What kind of subject/content is this?

Content Vertical
= In what production medium/lane is it expressed?

Content Unit / Publication
= What concrete thing did we make and publish?
```

These concepts MUST NOT collapse into one taxonomy.

## Features

- [Narrative Home](01_narrative-home.md) — operational command center for active Tracks, Beats, coverage, evidence and next actions.
- [Narrative Tracks](02_tracks.md) — durable audience-facing storylines with roles/purpose/themes/questions/disclosure rules.
- [Narrative Beats](03_beats.md) — concrete progression units inside Tracks.
- [Signature Differentiators](04_signature-differentiators.md) — cross-cutting recognizable execution/identity differentiators; not forced into a storyline.
- [Narrative Mix Plan & Coverage](05_mix-plan.md) — optional target vs actual storytelling mix without quotas.
- [Narrative Analytics](06_narrative-analytics.md) — track-level evidence with sample size, context, outliers and confidence discipline.

## Domain invariants

1. Narrative is subordinate to human-controlled Identity, not performance optimization.
2. Performance signal can create a hypothesis; it cannot auto-rewrite Identity or Narrative structure.
3. 50/30/20 is an optional heuristic, never a universal rule.
4. Narrative coverage and Content Pillar mix are separate.
5. No magic Narrative Score.
6. Missing data is missing, not zero.
7. Historical lineage survives Track/Beat/archive/Identity changes.
8. Protected canon never leaks through narrative recommendations or analytics.
